# Bar-Compliant Marketing Reviewer v1.0.1

Adds Legal Builder Hub freshness frontmatter (`freshness_category: regulatory`, 6-month window) with `verified_against` sources for the ABA Model Rules and each of the five bundled states (CA, TX, NY, FL, IL). No functional changes.

## What's included

### `/marketing-review` — Bar-Compliant Marketing Reviewer

Reviews attorney marketing materials — websites, social posts, ads, email campaigns — against your state bar's advertising rules and flags non-compliant language before it goes live.

## Setup

Install time: under 3 minutes. No connector setup — this plugin reads only the text or workspace folder you explicitly paste or attach.

## Compliance

Requires Claude for Work, Claude Team, or Claude Enterprise. Do not use a consumer Claude plan (Claude Pro or Personal) with confidential matter information. Every output carries the `AI-ASSISTED DRAFT — ATTORNEY REVIEW REQUIRED` header and `Not legal advice` footer.
